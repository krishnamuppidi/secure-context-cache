# Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We pledge to make participation in Secure Context Cache a harassment-free experience for everyone,
regardless of age, body size, disability, ethnicity, sex characteristics, gender identity and
expression, level of experience, education, socioeconomic status, nationality, personal
appearance, race, caste, color, religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

## Expected behavior

- Be respectful, technically specific, and open to correction.
- Critique ideas and evidence, not people.
- Protect private, confidential, and security-sensitive information.
- Label benchmark limits and avoid overstating adoption or results.
- Accept maintainers' decisions about project scope and safety.

## Unacceptable behavior

Harassment, intimidation, discriminatory language, personal attacks, doxxing, publication of
private information, and deliberate disruption are not acceptable.

## Enforcement

Report conduct concerns privately to `nagamuppidi1015@gmail.com`. Reports will be reviewed
confidentially and in good faith. Project maintainers may remove, edit, or reject comments,
commits, code, issues, and other contributions that violate this code, and may temporarily or
permanently restrict participation.
